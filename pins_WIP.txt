Section V: On Presentation and Decency
Principle 501:
Do not confuse something being eye-catching with being attractive. A pile of refuse in the middle of a cafeteria is undoubtedly eye-catching, but it is entirely devoid of attraction. Your clothing and your physical presentation speak volumes about your internal character before you ever open your mouth. If your style relies purely on immodesty, you will certainly capture the eyes of the public, but you will most likely fail to be attractive.
• The Balance of Self-Respect: The rejection of exhibitionism is not a demand for total erasure. A high-trust society expects its citizens to navigate presentation through common sense, rejecting both extremes of cultural decay: the colony demands neither the shameless exposure of a street walker nor the stifling, forced concealment of a burka.
• The Standard of Intent: True aesthetic appeal rests on the dignity of self-respect, order, and intentional presentation, not on desperate bids for raw attention or the total suppression of individuality. Dress cleanly, present yourself with baseline decency, and let your competence be what commands the room.

Article 407: The Sanctity of the Marital Contract and Balanced Allocation of Asset Dissolution
A marriage executed under the Martian zone of influence is a binding civil covenant recorded permanently on the state ledger. To eliminate systemic exploitation, prevent the monetization of oath-breaking, and maintain proportional justice, the dissolution of a marital contract operates under strict financial and fault parameters:
• The No-Fault Separation Baseline: If a marital contract is dissolved by mutual agreement or initiated by a single party without a proven statutory fault, the separation of joint property follows a strict, non-punitive financial metric. All marital assets, holdings, and resource shares shall be split in direct proportion to each party's historical income contribution throughout the duration of the union.
• The Absolute Alimony Prohibition: Under no circumstance—regardless of gender, financial disparity, or duration of the marriage—shall the state enforce or mandate ongoing alimony, spousal maintenance, or post-divorce resource transfers. The financial contract concludes absolutely upon the execution of the property split.
• The Scale-Tilt Standard for Proven Fault: If a dissolution is brought before a court or an interpretation jury and a severe, objective breach of the baseline marital covenant is forensically proven, the asset split shall intentionally tilt in favor of the aggrieved party. The grounds for this structural tilt are limited to documented adultery, physical abuse, or a Tier 3 capital conviction.
• The Remuneration Threshold (The Proportional Tilt Limit): While the aggrieved party is entitled to meaningful, state-enforced remuneration for a broken covenant, a marital breach shall never be utilized as a weapon to completely destroy a citizen's economic life.
	• Adultery Fault: For proven adultery, the asset split scale shall tilt by a mandatory, fixed 60/40 ratio in favor of the innocent spouse across all joint holdings.
	• Physical Abuse Fault: For proven physical abuse, the state's criminal code enforces a direct, strict-liability "eye for an eye" corporal punishment under Title XIII. Civically, the asset split scale shall tilt by a maximum 75/25 ratio in favor of the aggrieved spouse to account for injury and security costs. The at-fault party is heavily penalized but is left with a survival baseline to prevent complete economic erasure.
• Tier 2 and Tier 3 Status: A standard Tier 2 intermediate conviction (such as an independent financial or corporate infraction) carries its own independent state branding and lashes, but remains entirely immaterial to the marriage contract and does not constitute a valid reason for a fault-based asset tilt. A Tier 3 capital conviction automatically triggers terminal execution via the airlock, rendering the marriage void; the remaining estate is split symmetrically according to historical income to preserve the lineage of the innocent spouse and children.
