Article 108: Mandatory Presence and Vote Casting Obligation
Elected council members are obligated to be physically present for all votes. Representation of constituents is the fundamental act of elected office. No mechanism — 
proxy voting, paired voting, delegated voting, or any equivalent arrangement — that allows a vote to be cast or counted in a member's physical absence is permitted, with the 
sole exception defined below.

* The Three-Option Vote: Every vote cast by a council member shall be one of three options: Yes, No, or Abstain. Abstain is a deliberate, present, recorded choice not to cast a 
directional vote. It counts toward quorum. It does not interfere with the minimum threshold for a legally binding vote. It means: I am here, I choose not to direct this. 
That is a legitimate democratic choice and it is on the public record for every constituent to see.

* Absence and Quorum: A member who is not physically present when a vote is called does not vote and does not count toward quorum. A sufficient number of absences may prevent a 
legally binding vote from occurring. This is a structural consequence of the quorum requirement, not a defect to be corrected. A vote that cannot achieve quorum does not pass.

* The Weaponized Walkout Prohibition: Deliberately absenting oneself or coordinating with other members to absent themselves for the purpose of collapsing quorum and defeating 
a bill through procedural obstruction rather than legitimate voting constitutes an abuse of the democratic process. A single member walking out is a pattern to be monitored.
A coordinated walkout designed to torpedo a specific bill is fraud against the democratic process, prosecuted as a Tier 2 offense at minimum and escalating to Tier 3 where the 
coordination is systemic or involves coercion of other members.

* The Medical Absence Exception: Genuine, documented, verifiable physical incapacity excuses absence without further penalty beyond the uncast vote. There is no retroactive 
vote, no recorded intent, no vote cast after the fact. The vote occurred when it occurred. Medical absence means the member missed it. That is the full extent of the exception.

* The Off-Planet Representative Exception: Council members serving in formally designated diplomatic or representative roles off-planet may cast votes remotely, provided their 
vote arrives within one light-day of the vote being called, or within a communications window defined as reasonable by downstream statute where faster-than-light communications 
exist. Their vote is added to the tally upon arrival within the window. Outside the window, the vote is not counted. There is no extension of the window and no retroactive 
inclusion.

* The Pattern of Absence: A council member who establishes a pattern of absence from votes is failing their constituents on the public record. The Constitution encourages a 
low threshold for what constitutes a pattern — act fast, ask questions soon. Downstream statute shall define the specific trigger, but the constitutional expectation is that 
the bar is low and the response is immediate. Upon a finding of pattern absence: the member is immediately removed from office; permanently barred from holding any state 
employment, appointed position, or elected office; and subject to institutional shunning under Article 203 for a period of 30 Martian years. The colony's verdict on someone
who took the mandate and did not show up for it is not a gentle one.

Article 109: Executive Veto and Direct Democratic Escalation
The head of the governing council may veto any bill passed by the Martian Council. A veto does not kill the bill. It escalates the matter to a binding popular vote of the citizenry.

* The Popular Vote Mechanism: Every citizen eligible to vote under Article 003 may cast a digital ballot on the government-provided
platform. The available options are: Yes, No, Abstain, or Unfounded Veto Reasoning. The vote is open for exactly 10 Martian days 
from the moment the veto is publicly recorded. No minimum turnout, quorum, or percentage of eligible participants is required. 
The result is binding.

* Mandatory Citizen Notification: Upon the exercise of a veto, the state shall immediately notify all eligible voters through the 
official government channels that a popular vote has been called, stating the subject of the bill and the duration of the voting 
window.

* The Unfounded Veto Reasoning Option: Selecting this option is a formal rejection of the veto’s justification. It records the 
citizen’s judgment that the veto lacks substantive merit and that the bill should proceed   . If this option receives the plurality 
of votes cast, the veto is nullified and the bill is treated as passed.

* Mandatory Public Reasoning: At the exact moment a veto is exercised, the council head must submit a plain-language statement 
of the specific objection to the public ledger under Title XXXIII. A veto without such a statement is invalid and has no legal 
effect.

* Timing and Effect: The bill remains suspended during the 10-day popular vote window. Upon conclusion of the vote, the result 
takes immediate effect according to the outcome. There is no further council reconsideration or secondary veto.







Article 1904: Gene Edited Food Safety, Farmer Rights, and the Prove It Mandate
Gene edited food products are subject to the following mandatory framework before commercial sale or distribution is permitted.

* The Prove It Mandate: No gene edited food product may be sold, distributed, or brought into the food supply or zone of influence until it has completed a minimum 10 Martian 
year focused safety study. The study must be double-blind, peer reviewed, independently reproduced annually for the full duration, and published in full on the public ledger. 
The nutritional value and digestibility of the edited product must meet or exceed that of the unedited equivalent — gene editing for food must be a straight upgrade, 
not a lateral trade-off. Any product that demonstrably reduces nutritional value or digestibility relative to its unedited equivalent fails the mandate regardless of other 
benefits.

* The Executive Participation Requirement: The executives of the company seeking approval must personally consume the product under study conditions for the full 10 Martian 
year duration. They eat what they're selling. This is non-negotiable and non-delegable.

* The Disease Resistance Exception: Where a gene edit targets exclusively disease resistance, pest resistance without altering the product's nutritional profile,
maintaining equivalent nutritional values is sufficient — a strict upgrade is not required. The 10 year study, peer review, and executive participation requirements still 
apply in full.

* Farmer Replanting Rights: No corporation, patent holder, or rights owner may restrict, sue, or penalize a farmer for replanting crops grown from gene edited seed they have 
legitimately purchased or harvested. If a farmer uses the produce of a crop to replant, they replant. The biological act of a plant reproducing itself generates no corporate 
IP claim. No contract, licensing agreement, or terms of sale may override this right. Any instrument attempting to do so is void from inception.

* No Nutritional Regression: A gene edited food product that is later found — through ongoing study, new evidence, or independent research — to have regressed in nutritional 
value or to be causing adverse health effects shall be immediately withdrawn from the food supply pending re-evaluation. The original approval does not protect against 
subsequent findings. Should it be proven, that any nutritional value have regressed, it's distribution rights are to be pulled. Current stock can be sold. Should it be proven,
that it has adverse health effects, it is to be pulled inmediatly, destroyed and the company investigated if they knew. Regardless of that probe, they must pay for any damage,
should there be any for the citizens.
