Article 2107: The Absolute Right of Exclusion
The state retains an absolute, sovereign, and non-negotiable right to refuse entry, visa status, residency, or citizenship to any foreign national, individual, or organized demographic group for any reason, or for no publicly stated reason whatsoever.

* The Total Finality Standard: Border processing is a sovereign checkpoint, not a judicial court. The state is under zero constitutional obligation to provide due process, legal representation, or a stated justification to any non-citizen seeking entry.
Once an exclusion order is issued by a port authority, border marshal, or immigration desk, it is absolute, immediate, and completely un-appealable.

* Cultural Homogeneity Framework: Because a closed ecosystem possesses a zero-percent margin for social decay or parallel systems of tribal law, the state explicitly retains the right to discriminate at the border. Entry may be denied based directly on cultural background,
planet of origin, group habits, or any other demographic trait deemed incompatible with the colony's high-trust contract.

* The Discretionary Core: The state recognizes that structural subversion cannot always be detected by centralized intelligence databases. Localized port administrators and border marshals are empowered to exercise immediate, gut-level discretionary exclusion based on
behavioral observation. It is what it is.

Section V: On Presentation and Decency
Principle 501:
Do not confuse something being eye-catching with being attractive. A pile of refuse in the middle of a cafeteria is undoubtedly eye-catching, but it is entirely devoid of attraction. Your clothing and your physical presentation speak volumes about your internal character before you ever open your mouth. If your style relies purely on immodesty, you will certainly capture the eyes of the public, but you will most likely fail to be attractive.
• The Balance of Self-Respect: The rejection of exhibitionism is not a demand for total erasure. A high-trust society expects its citizens to navigate presentation through common sense, rejecting both extremes of cultural decay: the colony demands neither the shameless exposure of a street walker nor the stifling, forced concealment of a burka.
• The Standard of Intent: True aesthetic appeal rests on the dignity of self-respect, order, and intentional presentation, not on desperate bids for raw attention or the total suppression of individuality. Dress cleanly, present yourself with baseline decency, and let your competence be what commands the room.


Article 2805: Empirical Biological Diversity and Research Funding Integrity
The constitutional declaration that demographic concepts are legally non-existent to the state (Article 004) applies strictly to civil standing, voting eligibility, legal rights, and criminal accountability. This legal blindness shall never be weaponized to restrict,
censor, or defund empirical scientific inquiry into biological reality:

* The Scientific Recognition Standard: The state recognizes as a matter of raw, measurable data that distinct species, sub-races, and evolutionary offshoots possess inborn genetic variances, distinct physiological structures, and unique chemical or microbiological profiles.
Policy shall never force the colony to blind itself to an obvious empirical truth for the sake of political comfort or ideological uniformity.

* The Pathogen and Symbiote Protocol: The medical apparatus is explicitly mandated to track group-specific biological anomalies—including instances where a specific demographic group acts as a benign, symbiotic carrier of a pathogen that is entirely harmless to them but
catastrophic or devastating to the survival of other populations within the shared habitat. Tracking these variations is a life-critical logistical requirement, not an act of discrimination.

* The Investigation of Bias: Where public friction, cultural animosity, or systemic biases emerge regarding a specific offshoot population or group, the state shall not engage in performative moral crusades or blind suppression of the sentiment. Instead, the scientific and 
intelligence apparatus is directed to forensically analyze the root-cause data to determine precisely why the friction exists—distinguishing between hollow, lazy prejudice and valid, underlying biological or behavioral anomalies that threaten the colony's structural safety.
*
 The Allocation of Research Capital: Public funding, state medical grants, and clinical laboratory resources shall never be withheld from scientific research, pharmaceutical development, or genetic tracking projects simply because the study targets a race-specific or
species-specific biological vulnerability. Medical science operates on empirical data, not social policy.

* The Professional Domain: The determination of whether a medical study or genetic therapy project is biologically justified belongs entirely to the practicing field professionals and laboratory directors assigned to the work. The state is strictly prohibited from
micro-managing scientific definitions or enforcing ideological purity tests over clinical data.
